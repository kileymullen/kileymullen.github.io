Consumer Behavior · Class project Michelin Air View project · Expand +

For my Consumer Behavior class, my team and I developed Michelin Air, a subscription concept that uses scent to help travelers revisit memorable meals. Each collectible canister would be modeled after a Michelin-starred restaurant in Europe and capture the aromas of its dining room.

Our approach
We surveyed consumers about their travel habits, dining preferences, and interest in souvenirs. We also compared the concept with novelty souvenirs and home fragrance brands to explore how it could stand out.
What we learned
Most respondents valued sentimental travel keepsakes, and restaurant reputation strongly influenced their choices. Many preferred a smaller collectible, and respondents showed strong interest in redeeming a dining voucher.
